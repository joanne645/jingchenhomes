Why the listing can't answer this

Listing descriptions, map sites and neighbors do not decide which school a child attends. Public school assignment depends on the full street address, the district's boundaries and that year's placement, and city lines, ZIP codes and district lines all differ. Check before you write an offer. After closing is too late.

Three terms that get mixed up

TermWhat it means
School districtThe district an address belongs to. One address can have separate elementary and high school districts
Attendance boundaryThe area within a district assigned to a given school
Trustee areaA school board election zone, unrelated to school assignment

Watch the names as well. Fremont Unified serves the city of Fremont. Fremont Union High School District is a South Bay high school district with no connection to it.

How to look it up

  1. Confirm the district. In Santa Clara County, start with the county office of education's School District Locator, which points you to the Assessor's parcel information. Elsewhere, use the district's own tool, and call about any address near a boundary.
  2. Find the school. Enter the full address in the district's official locator. Our city guides list these tools, for example Fremont, Cupertino and San Jose. Check elementary, middle and high school separately.
  3. Ask about space. Contact the district with the address, the enrollment year and the grade, and ask whether that school has room.
  4. Keep a record. Save the result with a date, and get important answers in writing when you can.

A result is not a seat

This is the point most often misunderstood. Several Bay Area districts state that a locator result only shows the school tied to an address, and that a student may be placed elsewhere when that school is full. The terms vary, and you may see overload, overflow or diversion. The formal placement is usually set at enrollment.

Assignments change over time too. Bay Area districts redraw boundaries, and several San Jose area districts have closed or merged schools recently. Older maps and listings may be out of date, so check for the year your child would enroll.

Where to learn about a school

  • California School Dashboard: the state's accountability tool, with multiple measures and student group views. Note the report year and compare the same year.
  • SARC: each school's annual accountability report, covering class size, staffing and school climate.
  • The school itself: tours, parent nights and course information.

We don't rank schools or say which is better. A single score won't describe your child's experience, so read several measures together.

Common mistakes

  • Treating a choice program as a neighborhood school. Immersion and magnet programs usually need a separate application, and living next door gives no guarantee.
  • Stopping at the district without finding the specific school.
  • Missing registration timing. Some districts require you to have moved in before you enroll, so coordinate your closing and move.
  • Counting on a transfer. Space usually depends on the district placing its own assigned students first.
School and boundary information changes, and these notes are current as of November 2026. Rely on the district's confirmation for the relevant year. Nothing here promises that an address maps to, or a student can attend, any particular school.

Next step

Once you have a candidate home, work through the four steps with the full address. Then read the district's answer alongside the property's documents before you decide to offer.

95128 | West / Central San Jose: Valley Fair, the hospitals and the Bascom corridor

95128 sits between West and Central San Jose, where I-280 meets I-880, with Stevens Creek Blvd, Winchester Blvd and Bascom Ave as the main roads. Santana Row and Westfield Valley Fair are on the north side, and Santa Clara Valley Medical Center and O’Connor Hospital are both in this ZIP code, so shopping and medical care are closer at hand than in most residential areas. Housing is mixed: newer condos around Santana Row, condos and townhomes along the main roads, and mostly single-family homes on the residential streets. With that range, plus pockets of unincorporated county land, comparisons need to go down to the street.

What buyers should know: First confirm whether the address is inside city limits or on county land. Burbank, along Bascom Ave, is an unincorporated community surrounded by San Jose, and permits and services there go through the county. Schools here involve Campbell Union SD, Luther Burbank SD and Campbell Union HSD among others, so confirm the full address with the district. For condos, check the assigned parking, HOA dues, reserves and special assessments. Near Valley Fair and I-280, see the traffic on a weekend and at rush hour.

What sellers should know: Condos, townhomes and single-family homes should not be priced together, and condos near Santana Row belong in a different set from those elsewhere in the ZIP code. Gather permit records from the right agency, county or city, depending on the parcel. The city approved Urban Village plans for Santana Row/Valley Fair, Winchester and Stevens Creek in 2017, so when buyers ask about changes nearby, answer from the city’s published information.

West / Central San Jose in depth

Location and boundaries

95128 sits between West and Central San Jose, where I-280 meets I-880, with Stevens Creek Blvd, Winchester Blvd and Bascom Ave as the main roads. Westfield Valley Fair straddles the San Jose and Santa Clara city line. County land is mixed into the ZIP code: Burbank, along Bascom Ave between West San Carlos St and I-280, is an unincorporated community surrounded by San Jose, so a San Jose mailing address doesn’t prove a home is inside city limits. West San Jose and Central San Jose are customary names with no legal boundary.

Homes

Housing is very mixed: newer condos around Santana Row, condos and townhomes along Stevens Creek Blvd and Winchester Blvd, and mostly single-family homes on the residential streets, with ages to confirm by address. For a condo, check assigned parking, guest parking, elevator and common-area upkeep, what HOA dues cover, any special assessments or pending lawsuits, and whether lenders limit financing in that building. For a house, check permits for additions, the roof and plumbing; permit records for county parcels are held by the county.

Daily life

Shopping is concentrated on the north side, where open-air Santana Row sits across the street from indoor Westfield Valley Fair, and parking gets tight on weekends and holidays. Bascom Community Center and Bascom Branch Library are both at 1000 S Bascom Ave. Santa Clara Valley Medical Center (751 S Bascom Ave) is the county hospital, with an emergency department and trauma center listed on its site, and O’Connor Hospital (2105 Forest Ave) also has an emergency department. Living close to a hospital doesn’t mean your plan covers it, so check your network.

Commute and schools

I-280 heads northwest to Cupertino and east to Downtown, and I-880 goes north toward the East Bay and connects south to SR-17. VTA Rapid 523 follows the Stevens Creek corridor past Valley Fair and Santana Row, and the Green Line’s Fruitdale and Bascom stations are along Southwest Expressway to the south. Several school districts are involved: Monroe Middle (1055 S Monroe St) in Campbell Union School District, Del Mar High (1224 Del Mar Ave) in Campbell Union HSD, and Luther Burbank School District (4 Wabash Ave, K–8) are all in this ZIP code. Confirm every school by full address, using the county Locator to find the district and then the district’s own tool for the specific school.

The trade-offs

The location is central, with freeways, hospitals and shopping all close. The trade-offs are heavy holiday traffic around Valley Fair and Santana Row and noise along I-280 and I-880. In August 2017 the city approved Urban Village plans for Santana Row/Valley Fair, Winchester and Stevens Creek, so more mid-rise housing and commercial space may come to those corridors. County parcels and neighboring city streets use different channels for permits and services, so homes that look alike can involve different paperwork.

What to look for on a visit

On a weekend afternoon, drive Stevens Creek Blvd to Winchester Blvd and see the traffic and curb parking, then on a weekday morning drive from the home to the I-280 or I-880 on-ramp. At a home near a freeway or main road, spend extra time in the yard or on the balcony listening. If you plan to use transit, walk to the Rapid 523 stop or Green Line station. Before you offer, use County Assessor records to confirm whether the address is in the city or on county land.

95118 | Cambrian / South San Jose: postwar homes where school districts meet

95118 sits in southwest San Jose between Cambrian and Blossom Valley, with Blossom Hill Rd, Branham Ln and Meridian Ave as the everyday roads and SR-85, SR-87 and Almaden Expressway close by. Most homes are single-story ranches from the 1950s through the 1970s on regular lots, usually with two-car garages, and Blossom Valley adds scattered townhomes from the 1970s and 1980s. Vineland Branch Library is on Blossom Hill Rd, Oakridge to the southeast handles bigger shopping trips, and getting around mostly means driving. What sets this ZIP code apart is that several school districts meet here, so neighborhoods with similar names can fall under different school systems.

What buyers should know: Put schools first on your list. Pioneer High is in San José Unified and Branham High is in Campbell Union HSD, and both campuses are in 95118, while elementary and middle schools here also involve San José Unified, Union School District and others. Confirm the full address with the district rather than relying on a listing. On older ranch homes, look at the original sewer line, single-pane windows, roof, HVAC and permits for additions.

What sellers should know: Group comparable sales by school district first, then by home type and lot. Homes a few blocks apart may belong to different districts, and a 1970s townhome should not sit in the same comparison set as a detached house. Have dates for roof, plumbing and sewer work ready along with permits for additions, and keep school information in the listing to the district and its official lookup.

Cambrian / South San Jose in depth

Location and boundaries

95118 is in southwest San Jose between Cambrian and Blossom Valley, with Blossom Hill Rd, Branham Ln and Meridian Ave as the everyday roads. The city’s Planning Area is called Cambrian/Pioneer and reaches east to around Almaden Expy. Cambrian and Blossom Valley are customary names with no legal boundary, and they do not define school attendance areas. Some parcels around Cambrian are unincorporated Santa Clara County land that still uses a San Jose mailing address, so confirm which applies before buying.

Homes

Most homes are single-story ranches from the 1950s through the 1970s on regular lots, usually with two-car garages, and Blossom Valley has scattered townhomes from the 1970s and 1980s. On the houses, the usual checks are how far the original sewer line, single-pane windows, roof and HVAC have been updated. Many older homes have been expanded, so verify the permits. Townhomes trade stairs, tandem garages and small yards for less upkeep, and the HOA documents deserve a full read.

Daily life

Vineland Branch Library is at 1450 Blossom Hill Rd, De Anza Park is at Meridian Ave and Princeton Dr, and Branham Park and Scottsdale Park are along Branham Ln. Bigger shopping trips go southeast to Oakridge, around Blossom Hill Rd and Santa Teresa Blvd, and Almaden Lake Park is south along Almaden Expy. Good Samaritan Hospital (2425 Samaritan Dr) to the west has a 24-hour emergency department; ask your insurer whether it is in your network.

Commute and schools

Drivers mostly rely on SR-85, SR-87 and Almaden Expy, with SR-87 running north to Downtown and the airport and SR-85 heading northwest to Cupertino and Mountain View. Light rail means going east to the Blue Line’s Ohlone/Chynoweth or Blossom Hill stations, and Caltrain’s Blossom Hill station has only the infrequent South County Connector trains. Schools cross several systems: Pioneer High (1290 Blossom Hill Rd) is in San José Unified, Branham High (1570 Branham Ln) is in Campbell Union HSD, and Lietz Elementary and Dartmouth Middle are in Union School District, while some addresses around Blossom Valley are in Oak Grove School District, with high school in East Side Union HSD. San José Unified’s new boundaries apply to incoming students from 2027–28, so confirm every school by full address with the district.

The trade-offs

Homes here are practical and lots are regular, but daily life runs on a car, and reaching rail starts with a drive. Streets near SR-85 and SR-87 get freeway noise. School districts change by street, so two similar homes can have entirely different schools. VTA’s June 2026 materials list a housing and commercial project at Blossom Hill station with a first phase expected in 2028–2029, so expect construction around there.

What to look for on a visit

During the weekday morning rush, drive from the home to both the SR-85 and SR-87 on-ramps and compare. At dismissal time, pass by the assigned schools and watch the drop-off traffic and crossings. On a weekend, visit Vineland Branch Library and the nearest shopping center and note how parking works. While you’re at the property, run the address through the county Locator and the district’s own tool.

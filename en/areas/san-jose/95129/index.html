95129 | West San Jose: what sits behind the prices

95129 is one of West San Jose’s higher-priced ZIP codes, next to Cupertino, Saratoga and the job centers around Apple, with I-280 along the north and Lawrence Expressway, Saratoga Ave and Bollinger Rd as the everyday roads. Most homes are 1950s and 1960s single-story ranches with three or four bedrooms and two-car garages, many of them remodeled or expanded with a second floor, and condos and townhomes line the main roads. Calabazas Branch Library, Calabazas Park, Rainbow Park and Saratoga Creek Park are all in the area, and shopping runs along Saratoga Ave, De Anza Blvd and Stevens Creek Blvd. Buyers here are usually weighing a combination of location, school assignment, land value and long-term plans rather than square footage alone.

What buyers should know: A 95129 address does not automatically mean Cupertino schools. Elementary, middle and high school districts can change from one street to the next. Two homes of similar size can price very differently because of school boundaries, the lot, room to expand and nearby traffic.

What sellers should know: The ZIP-wide median is not a pricing tool. Start with recent sales inside the same school boundary, with a similar lot, age and level of finish.

West San Jose in depth

Location and boundaries

West San Jose is the far western part of the city, surrounded by Cupertino, Saratoga, Campbell and Santa Clara, with I-280 to the north and Lawrence and San Tomas expressways running north-south through it. Saratoga Ave, Stevens Creek Blvd, Bollinger Rd and Prospect Rd are the main roads. The city’s Planning Area is called West Valley, while listings and everyday speech use West San Jose, covering ZIP codes including 95129 and 95117. It is neither a separate city nor a legally defined area. Homes here have a San Jose mailing address and get San Jose city services, yet may fall in school districts with Cupertino or Campbell in their names, so keep those three things separate.

Homes

Most homes are 1950s and 1960s single-story ranches with three or four bedrooms, two-car garages and regular lots, and many have been remodeled or had a second story added. Condos and townhomes line Saratoga Ave, Stevens Creek Blvd and Winchester Blvd, and the Santana Row area has newer high-density condos. On older ranches, verify permits for additions and check the roof, plumbing and electrical panel. If you come across a midcentury modern home with a flat roof, large panes of glass and radiant heat in the slab, hire an inspector who knows that type of construction.

Daily life

Public facilities fall into a few clusters. On the southwest side near Cupertino, Calabazas Branch Library (1230 S Blaney Ave) and Calabazas Park share a street, Rainbow Park (1295 Johnson Ave) has a neighborhood center, and Lynbrook High School is on Johnson Ave as well. In the middle are Saratoga Creek Park (5293 Hoyet Dr) with its adjacent dog park, and Murdock Park (1188 Wunderlich Dr). To the east, the San Tomas area has West Valley Branch Library (1243 San Tomas Aquino Rd) and Starbird Park (1050 Boynton Ave), and Cypress Community Center is at 403 Cypress Ave. Shopping options are plentiful: Santana Row and Valley Fair on the north edge, Westgate Center on Saratoga Ave, and a number of Japanese, Korean and Chinese supermarkets along Saratoga Ave, De Anza Blvd and Stevens Creek Blvd; confirm which stores are open now. El Paseo de Saratoga, at Saratoga Ave and Lawrence Expy, is being rebuilt. In December 2024 the city approved a revised plan with more than 700 apartments, a senior care facility and a Whole Foods, and an August 2025 report showed the older shops on the west side being demolished, with the full project expected to take nearly four years.

Commute and schools

By car, I-280 heads north to Cupertino, Sunnyvale and the Peninsula, while Lawrence and San Tomas expressways go north to US-101 and North San Jose and south to SR-85 or SR-17. There is no Caltrain or BART station in the area, so a rail commute starts with a trip to Santa Clara, Sunnyvale or Diridon. Schools are the easiest thing to get wrong here, with at least five districts involved. On the Cupertino side, elementary and middle grades are in Cupertino Union School District and high schools in Fremont Union High School District, and Lynbrook High itself is in San Jose (1280 Johnson Ave); note that this is the South Bay’s Fremont Union, unrelated to Fremont Unified in the city of Fremont. In the middle and east, elementary and middle grades are in Moreland School District or Campbell Union School District, with high schools in Campbell Union High School District, such as Prospect or Westmont. The same neighborhood name, or even two sides of a single street, can fall in different districts. Check in this order: use the county School District Locator, which points to the County Assessor’s parcel records, to find the district, then use that district’s own lookup for the specific school. Cupertino Union says its lookup gives only a preliminary assignment, with the final placement made at registration, and Fremont Union also advises relying on the county lookup for decisions such as buying or renting.

The trade-offs

West San Jose’s appeal is its central location and easy shopping. What needs care is that school districts change by street, and some streets belong to districts with Cupertino in the name, so you have to confirm a listing’s school information yourself by address. Lawrence and San Tomas expressways and I-280 bring noise, and holiday traffic around Santana Row and Valley Fair is heavy. The area near El Paseo will be under construction for the next few years, and traffic and retail there will change once it’s finished.

What to look for on a visit

Bring the address and, standing at the property, look it up on your phone in both the County Assessor tool and the relevant district lookup. On a weekday evening, drive Saratoga Ave from I-280 to Lawrence Expy to feel the traffic, and stop by the El Paseo construction site. On a weekend, visit Calabazas Park and Saratoga Creek Park to compare the streets in the southwest and central parts. If the home is near an expressway, spend extra time in the backyard listening.

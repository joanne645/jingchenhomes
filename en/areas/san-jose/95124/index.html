95124 | Cambrian: classic single-family homes and a workable commute

95124 covers much of the Cambrian area, bordering Campbell and Los Gatos, with SR-85 and SR-17 to the south and west and Camden Ave, Union Ave, Hillsdale Ave and Bascom Ave as the main local roads. These are established low-rise neighborhoods, mostly single-story ranch homes built in tracts in the 1950s and 1960s, typically with three or four bedrooms and two-car garages on regular lots. Cambrian Branch Library, Camden Community Center and Houge Park are spread through the area, Good Samaritan Hospital is on Samaritan Dr, and most errands happen by car. Some parcels are unincorporated county land even though they carry a San Jose mailing address. Buyers usually compare classic single-family homes, yard space, how much updating a home needs, and the commute.

What buyers should know: Look hard at the roof, drainage, plumbing, electrical and foundation, and confirm that any additions were permitted. A new kitchen and floors do not tell you those things. School boundaries inside Cambrian also need checking address by address.

What sellers should know: For classic homes here, a pre-listing inspection, needed repairs, sensible staging and a sharp set of comparable sales usually do more than a general “great schools” pitch.

Cambrian in depth

Location and boundaries

Cambrian is in southwest San Jose, bordering Campbell and Los Gatos to the west, with SR-85 and SR-17 passing on the south and west sides and Camden Ave, Union Ave, Hillsdale Ave and Bascom Ave as the main local roads. The city’s Planning Area is called Cambrian/Pioneer and reaches east to around Almaden Expy, while locals usually use Cambrian or Cambrian Park for ZIP code 95124 and part of 95118. One thing to watch: some parcels here are still unincorporated Santa Clara County land. They use a San Jose mailing address, but the county handles building permits, policing and some municipal services, so confirm which applies through the County Assessor or city GIS before you buy.

Homes

The core of the housing stock is single-story ranch homes built in tracts in the 1950s and 1960s, with three or four bedrooms, two-car garages, regular lots and quiet streets, plus some condos and townhomes along the main roads. Many of these homes have been expanded or remodeled, so verify permits for any added square footage and look at the roof, original plumbing, electrical panel capacity and foundation ventilation. For homes on county parcels, permit records are held by the county, and sidewalks and streetlights may differ from the city street next door.

Daily life

Facilities are spread out, and most errands are done by car. Camden Community Center is at 3369 Union Ave, with a pool and park at the same address, and Cambrian Branch Library is at 1780 Hillsdale Ave. Houge Park (3952 Twilight Dr) has a neighborhood center, Paul Moore Park is on Hillsdale Ave, and Doerr Park is at Potrero Dr and Park Wilshire Dr. The Los Gatos Creek Trail runs to the northwest, with city data listing segments from Meridian Ave to Leigh Ave and from Leigh Ave to South Bascom Ave. Good Samaritan Hospital at 2425 Samaritan Dr has a 24-hour emergency department; ask your insurer whether it is in your network. Cambrian Park Plaza, the 1953 shopping center at Camden and Union, was approved by the City Council in 2022 for redevelopment as a mixed-use community. As of KQED’s April 2026 report, work had not started, the developer was asking to adjust the permit so housing could go first, the city was reviewing that request, and the current permit expires in 2028.

Commute and schools

By car, SR-17 heads north to I-880 or I-280, and SR-85 heads northwest toward Cupertino and Mountain View. There is no rail station in the area, so reaching light rail or Caltrain starts with a drive. Schools are what most need checking by address here. Elementary and middle grades split between Cambrian School District (Bagby, Fammatre, Farnham and Price Middle, plus Steindorf STEAM Magnet, which requires a separate application) and Union School District (Carlton, Noddin, Oster, Lietz, Union Middle, Dartmouth Middle and others). High schools are in Campbell Union High School District, mainly Leigh and Branham in this area, and the Pioneer side of the planning area to the east moves into San José Unified. All three districts have their own address lookups, and Campbell Union HSD states on its website that its boundary map is the only official basis for high school assignment, so don’t rely on a listing description or an agent’s word.

The trade-offs

Cambrian’s homes are practical and the neighborhoods are established. What you accept is needing a car for most things, no central walkable shopping street, and school districts that can change from one block to the next. Streets near SR-85 and SR-17 get freeway noise. Near Cambrian Park Plaza, neighbors also live with uncertainty about redevelopment: the site could stay as it is for a long time or go into construction within a few years.

What to look for on a visit

On a weekday morning, drive from the home to the SR-85 and SR-17 on-ramps and compare the routes. At school dismissal time, stop by the school assigned to that address and watch the drop-off traffic and crossings. Pull over at Camden and Union to see the current state of Cambrian Park Plaza. If you come across a street without sidewalks, look up whether it is a county parcel.

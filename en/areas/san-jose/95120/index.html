95120 | Almaden Valley: space, hill views and holding costs

Photo needed
Foothill neighborhood panorama (wide)
  • $2.00M median sale price
  • 11 days on market
  • 46 sales

Almaden Valley sits at the south end of San Jose, near the foothills and open space. Buyers tend to focus on home size, yards and the neighborhood setting, and on the commute trade-off to Silicon Valley’s main job centers.

What buyers should know: Check insurance availability, foothill fire risk, drainage, retaining walls, the roof and upkeep costs. Conditions vary a lot by location and property, so the whole ZIP cannot be treated as one risk level.

What sellers should know: Separate standard homes, large-lot properties, view homes and high-end remodels. With few high-end sales, a single month’s median can move simply because of which homes sold.

Realtor.com’s October 2026 data shows a median sold price near $2.03M and a median list price near $2.18M. These measure different sets of homes, so the gap is not negotiating room. Realtor.com

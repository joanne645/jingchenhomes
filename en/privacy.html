Privacy notice

How we handle information you send through this site.

What we collect

When you send the contact form, we receive the name, phone, email, topic, city of interest and message you enter, plus the page address and referring page.

How we use it

We use this information only to contact you and follow up on your real estate inquiry. We do not sell your personal information.

Storage and third parties

Form submissions are stored in a Supabase database and sent to Jing through an email service. The site is hosted on Vercel and uses Google Fonts. City page maps load data from OpenStreetMap and the U.S. Census Bureau, and these services may receive technical information such as your IP address. Some sold-home images load from third-party listing sites.

Changes or deletion

To review, correct or delete information you sent, call 925-917-4019.

Updates

This notice may change. Last updated November 2026.

Where Your Budget Goes

Based on the median prices above

$1.3M – $1.6M

Median sale prices in San Jose, Milpitas, Dublin, Fremont, San Ramon and Pleasanton fall roughly in this range — one of the more accessible tiers in the Bay Area.

$1.7M – $2M

Sunnyvale and Mountain View medians fall in this range, closer to major tech employers with an easier commute.

$3M and above

Cupertino and Palo Alto medians are above $3M, typically reflecting top-rated schools and prime locations.

These ranges are a rough summary of each city’s 3-month median sale price. Prices still vary widely by neighborhood, lot size and home age within the same city, so this is no substitute for planning around your actual budget and target areas. Cities & Neighborhoods

Palo Alto

Next to Stanford with highly regarded public schools — one of the most central parts of Silicon Valley, and the most expensive.

Cupertino

Home to Apple’s headquarters, with highly rated schools and a community of tech professionals and families.

Sunnyvale

Dense with tech companies and easy to commute from, with prices somewhat friendlier than Palo Alto or Cupertino.

Mountain View

Home to Google’s headquarters, with a lively downtown on Castro Street and a higher share of condos and townhouses.

San Jose

The largest city in the South Bay, spanning everything from affordable to high-end neighborhoods — the widest range of options.

Fremont

In the southern East Bay, family-oriented, with many recent new developments and value between the South Bay and East Bay.

Milpitas

The most affordable city in this group, with many new condo and townhouse projects and an easy commute to the South Bay.

Dublin

One of the fastest-growing Tri-Valley cities, with many new communities and generally newer, larger homes.

Pleasanton

A Tri-Valley city known for well-regarded schools and a more relaxed pace than the South Bay.

San Ramon

Mostly master-planned communities with several major company offices — a good fit for families who value planned neighborhoods.

These are general descriptions of each city. For school ratings, neighborhood details and active listings, it’s best to look at the latest data — book a consultation for a closer comparison of the cities you’re considering.

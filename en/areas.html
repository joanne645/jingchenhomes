10 CITIES

A guide for each of 10 cities

Each guide covers the city, neighborhoods, home types, price data, commuting, official school lookups, daily life, recent sales and related articles. English guides are concise editions; the most detailed versions are in Chinese.

Palo Alto

Two Caltrain stations and two walkable main streets, with older neighborhoods, Eichler tracts and the Baylands in one city.

Median sale $3.52M

Cupertino

Against the foothills with no rail station; daily life runs along Stevens Creek Blvd, mostly by car.

Median sale $3.06M

Sunnyvale

Two Caltrain stations plus light rail in the north; school districts and commute directions differ from north to south.

Median sale $1.75M

Mountain View

Two Caltrain stations and the light-rail terminus, pedestrian Castro Street, with Shoreline and office campuses north of 101.

Median sale About $1.9M – $1.96M

San Jose

Large and varied: choose a living area by office location and home type first, then compare streets.

Median sale $1.40M

Fremont

Two BART stations and the Dumbarton Bridge; each of its seven areas commutes in a different direction.

Median sale $1.50M

Milpitas

BART and light rail share one station; newer townhomes near it, older single-family streets, and the east foothills.

Median sale $1.32M

Dublin

North of I-580 with two BART stations; older neighborhoods to the west, newer communities with large parks to the east.

Median sale $1.32M

Pleasanton

Historic Main Street and a Saturday farmers’ market, two BART stations on the north edge, plus ACE trains.

Median sale $1.60M

San Ramon

Along I-680 with Bishop Ranch as its office and retail core; there is no BART station inside the city.

Median sale $1.57M

BUDGET

Where a budget can go

$1.3M – $1.6M

Median sale prices in San Jose, Milpitas, Dublin, Fremont, San Ramon and Pleasanton fall roughly in this range.

$1.7M – $2M

Sunnyvale and Mountain View medians fall in this range.

$3M and above

Cupertino and Palo Alto medians are above $3M.

These ranges are a rough summary of the medians above. Prices vary widely within a city by neighborhood, lot size and age, so this is no substitute for planning around your own budget and target areas.
